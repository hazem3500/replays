## Make a recording

Open **App → Recorder → Playground (end-to-end) → Manual**. You’re in a small Pinecone checkout. Select Record, consent, fill in the delivery form, simulate a problem, stop, and send. Mock mode compresses the actual rrweb recording and lets you watch it immediately in the real Viewer.

The **Guided** story does that for you and checks the payload. **Server upload** sends to your own Replays API. The existing Recorder UI-state stories show each moment over the same checkout.

## A few useful tools

Use **Theme** for the app’s light and dark palettes. **Viewport** gives you 320, 390, 768 and 1280px. Backgrounds, measure and outline are built into Storybook 10. Open **Accessibility** for axe findings and **Interactions** to follow a play function step by step. The Vitest addon runs these stories in Chromium.

## Real components, repeatable data

The six small recording fixtures are generated by `scripts/demo-seed.mjs` and checked into `stories/fixtures/data`. They contain real rrweb snapshots and typed diagnostic tracks. API responses are scoped to each story and restored afterwards. No production source changes are required.

See `docs/storybook.md` for commands, proxy setup, fixture conventions and the few app states that need a new prop or export to become independently renderable.
