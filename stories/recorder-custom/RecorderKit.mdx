# Build your own recorder UI

Your app owns the question and its wording. The headless recorder owns capture, review, uploads, privacy facts, and lifecycle cleanup.

Use `createGuidedRecorder` from `/recorder/ui` for the complete built-in flow. For your design system, use `RecorderProvider` from `/recorder/react` and copy `examples/recorder-shadcn/src`.

- `useRecorderPrompt`: consent facts, review summary, and indicator context.
- `useRecorderActions`: accept, decline, stop, send, discard, retry, dismiss.
- `useRecordingTimer`: elapsed and remaining labels, 30/10-second warnings.
- `useStopAttestation`: attach to the visible Stop button.
- `RecorderRegion`: excludes recorder chrome from capture.
- `/recorder/kit`: focus trap, focus return, live-region announcer, privacy selectors.
- `/recorder/testing`: `testRecorderUi` checks the contract using your test runner.

The custom stories use fake rrweb and upload transports. Consent, Stop, Send, Retry, and Dismiss remain interactive. Upload progress can be indeterminate when a transport cannot measure it.
